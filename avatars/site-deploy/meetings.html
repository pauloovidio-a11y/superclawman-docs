FAQ

Questions, answered

I already use an AI note-taker. Why add this?

Keep it if you like it. A note-taker writes down what was said, for you. SuperClawman takes part: it answers the room when you ask, keeps one memory per client, and drafts the recap to the attendees after the call, plus the proposal when you ask.

Which meeting platforms does it support?

Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams. It joins from your calendar automatically, or from any meeting link you send it.

Do the other participants know it's there?

Yes, by design. It joins as a named participant and announces itself when it enters. Transparency is part of the product, not a setting.

What languages does it work in?

Live voice works in English, Portuguese and Spanish on every plan. English and Portuguese can even mix in the same call. Briefs and recaps follow the language of the meeting.

What happens if I go over my meeting-hours?

It keeps joining. It never leaves a call because of the limit. At 80% you get an email. Past 100% we offer extra hours at $4 each, or a bigger plan. Nothing is charged without your OK.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes, any time, on your own. The billing link in your welcome email lets you switch plans or cancel in two clicks. If you cancel, your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

Can it really write a proposal from a call?

Yes, on Pro and Full Seat. After a sales call, ask for the proposal and it drafts one based on what was actually said: the pains, the numbers, the commitments. You polish it and send it. This exact flow closed our first real engagement.

Where does my meeting data live?

In dedicated storage for your account, not mixed with anyone else's. Per-account journals are exportable, and we delete everything on request. Enterprise engagements can run fully on infrastructure you own.
