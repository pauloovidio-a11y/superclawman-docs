FAQ

Questions, answered

Which meeting platforms does it support?

Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams. It joins from your calendar automatically, or from any meeting link you send it.

Do the other participants know it's there?

Yes — by design. It joins as a named participant and announces itself when it enters. Transparency is part of the product, not a setting.

What languages does it work in?

Live voice works in English and Portuguese today — including both in the same call. Briefs and recaps follow the language of the meeting. Spanish voice is available on request for founding customers.

What happens if I go over my monthly meeting-hours?

Nothing breaks — we'll reach out to right-size your plan. Founding customers get flexibility while we learn your real usage together.

Why only 10 founding seats?

Each seat is a dedicated, white-glove-provisioned agent, not a self-serve account. We cap the founding cohort so every customer gets direct attention from the team while we scale the platform. Founding pricing is locked for as long as you stay.

Can it really write a proposal from a call?

Yes. After a sales-shaped conversation, ask for the proposal and it drafts one grounded in what was actually said — the pains, the numbers, the commitments. You polish and send. This exact flow closed our first real engagement.

Where does my meeting data live?

In dedicated storage for your account, not mixed with anyone else's. Per-account journals are exportable, and we delete everything on request. Enterprise engagements can run fully on infrastructure you own.
