Questions

The ones worth asking

Do you see my messages?

No. Capture, indexing and reasoning all happen on your machine using your own AI subscription. We run no server in the path — there is nothing on our side to leak or subpoena.

Is this against WhatsApp's rules?

It links as a companion device, the same mechanism WhatsApp Web and Desktop use, and it reads your own messages. It never sends messages, never contacts anyone, and does no bulk or automated outreach — the behaviours that get accounts restricted. It's your data, read by software you run.

How far back does it go?

As far as your phone still holds. In our benchmark that was 17 months and 6,267 messages. We deliberately never promise "all your history" — we promise everything your device still has, and you'll see the exact number during setup.

Does it answer people for me?

No, and that's deliberate. It reads and answers you. Nothing is ever sent to a contact, which is what keeps your number safe and this simple.

Claude Code or Codex — which?

Either. The tools are exposed over MCP, so any assistant that speaks MCP can use them. Claude Code is what we develop against day to day.

What does "12 months of updates" mean?

WhatsApp's internals shift, and things break. Your purchase covers the current major version plus every update we ship for 12 months. After that it keeps working — you just stop receiving new versions unless you upgrade.

Why is it one-time and not a subscription?

Because the honest shape of the value is front-loaded. The first scan surfaces a year of backlog; the second month finds far less. We'd rather charge once for what it genuinely does than bill monthly for a declining return.

What does "founding" mean?

The first 20 buyers get set up personally, on a call with us, and their feedback shapes what ships next. It's capped because that attention doesn't scale.
