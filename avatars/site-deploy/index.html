What it does

Eight jobs, one assistant

All of it happens on WhatsApp. No new app to learn.

01

WhatsApp memory

Your WhatsApp history becomes searchable. Ask a question and get the answer with the conversation quoted. Voice notes are transcribed.

02

Calendar and routines

It reads your calendar, builds your day and warns you before each commitment. It runs routines: a morning summary, a deadline reminder, a list of open items.

03

Research with an answer

It searches the web and comes back with the answer, not a list of links: a news item, a supplier, a price, a company and who owns it.

04

Documents

It opens what you receive (PDF, spreadsheet, Word, screenshot) and builds a spreadsheet, a presentation or a document that is ready to forward.

05

Messages to other people

It speaks only with the people you allow. It confirms a reservation, tells the family group, follows up on a pending answer.

06

Travel

It quotes flights, hotels and car rental, and sends you the ride link ready to tap.

07

E-mail and cloud files

It reads your e-mail and your files. A personal Gmail is ready in two steps. A company e-mail needs your IT team to allow access.

08

Voice, alerts and learning

It answers by voice note when you ask. It tells you at once when something changes. A request it cannot do yet comes to our team.

Plain facts

What it does not do

We prefer that you know before you buy.

  • It does not buy or pay. It quotes and prepares. You confirm.
  • It does not hold your bank, broker or sensitive passwords.
  • It does not make phone calls.
  • It does not run mass campaigns or broadcast lists.
  • It does not answer your business's customers. That is a different agent, with a custom quote.
FAQ

Questions people ask before they start

Why do I need a Claude subscription?

Your assistant thinks with Claude, the AI made by Anthropic. The subscription is in your name and on your card, and you pay Anthropic direct. We do not resell it and we do not charge it. Your AI account stays yours.

Is Claude Pro enough?

Yes. Claude Pro is the minimum and it is enough to start. If you use your assistant all day, a larger Claude plan gives it more room. You change your plan with Anthropic at any time.

What does the monthly price cover?

Your own server, the installation, the connection of your accounts, 24-hour monitoring and support. Support means upgrades, fixes for any issue, new features and your requests. The only other cost is your Claude subscription.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

No setup fee and no lock-in. You pay month by month.

How long until my assistant is live?

About one week from your yes. Two things take the longest: the Claude subscription and, for a company e-mail, the approval of your IT team.

Do I need a new app or a new phone number?

No new app. You talk to your assistant on WhatsApp. It can work on your own number or on a second number. We decide that with you during setup.

Will it send messages to people without my permission?

No. It writes only to the people you allow, and it signs as your assistant. Everyone else gets nothing until you say so.

Where is my data, and who can see it?

Your data stays on a server that is only yours. No other customer shares it. Our team reaches it only to operate and support your assistant.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Portuguese and Spanish. It answers in the language you write in.

What is the difference between Personal and Business?

It is the same assistant. Personal is for one person and their own accounts. Business adds your work: company e-mail and files, messages to clients, suppliers and team, documents with your brand, and an invoice to your company.

How do I pay?

By card, once a month, in US dollars, through a secure Stripe link. SUPERCLAWMAN LLC sends the invoice.

How do I cancel?

Send us a message at any time. The month in progress is due. We hand you your data and delete your assistant from our servers.

What if I need more than one assistant?

Ask for a custom quote. We build assistants for a whole team, agents that answer customers, and integrations with company systems.
